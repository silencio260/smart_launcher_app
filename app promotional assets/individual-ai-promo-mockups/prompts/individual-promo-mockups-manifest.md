# Smart Launcher Individual Promo Mockups

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, using the provided Smart Launcher brief and launcher Play Store reference direction as style inspiration. The supplied screenshots were treated only as context; they were not pasted into these promotional images.

## Direction

- Format: landscape Google Play promotional screenshot style.
- Original generated finals: `app promotional assets/individual-ai-promo-mockups/finals/`
- Store-ready resized exports: `app promotional assets/individual-ai-promo-mockups/finals/play-store-ready/`
- Store-ready size: `1052x592`
- Preview sheet: `app promotional assets/individual-ai-promo-mockups/finals/individual-promo-mockups-preview-contact-sheet.jpg`
- Visual style: bright ASO-ready launcher promo cards, bold benefit headline, generated Android phone mockup, feature chips, warm GenRevibes orange accents.
- Privacy and clock UI style: mostly monochrome black and white inside the phone mockups, with orange used in surrounding promotional design.
