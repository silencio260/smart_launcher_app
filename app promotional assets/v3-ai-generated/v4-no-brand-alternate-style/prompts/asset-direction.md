# V4 No Brand Alternate Style

## Purpose

This folder is a small alternate exploration inspired by the older `v3-ai-generated` assets. It is not the default style for the main Play screenshot set.

## Style

- Brighter glossy app-store layouts.
- Light backgrounds with glassy colorful gradient accents.
- Multiple phone mockups and floating feature chips.
- No app identity or company branding.

## Required Brand-Safe Rules

- Do not include app name.
- Do not include app logo.
- Do not include company/developer name.
- Do not include platform logos, competitor marks, third-party app names, or recognizable social media icons.
- Use generic feature-led copy only.
